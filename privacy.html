Privacy Policy

This starter policy explains the intended data practices for ModelMetric Lab. Review it with your actual analytics, advertising, hosting, and legal setup before publishing.

Calculator inputs

The calculators in this starter site perform their core calculations in the browser. The site is not designed to require an account for normal tool use.

Analytics and advertising

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to measure page usage and improve the site. Vercel may process usage data according to its Privacy Policy. After advertising services are enabled, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising. Users can manage personalized advertising through Google’s Ads Settings.

Consent

For visitors in regions where consent is required, configure an appropriate consent management solution before serving personalized ads. Google currently requires a certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework for personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. See the current Google requirements before launch.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact address published on the Contact page.

Policy maintenance

Update this page whenever your analytics, advertising, cookies, vendors, or data flows materially change. This page is a template, not legal advice.
